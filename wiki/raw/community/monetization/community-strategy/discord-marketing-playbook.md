# Discord and Community Marketing Playbook

Roblox's Discover algorithm is organic-only. Paid ads can cold-start
you but don't feed back into your ranking signal. That makes
**off-platform communities** — Discord, TikTok, YouTube, content
creator partnerships — your highest-leverage growth lever.

## Why Discord specifically

- Persistent community (unlike TikTok / YT, which are one-shot).
- Direct line to your most engaged players.
- Free to run (Nitro for emoji is optional).
- Natively supports role-based perk distribution (e.g. role-gated
  promo codes, beta access).
- Ideal for distributing patch notes, running events, Q&A.

## Discord server structure (proven layout)

```
#welcome            - rules, auto-role, FAQ
#announcements      - one-way patch notes, events, AMAs
#community
  #general          - open chat
  #introductions    - new member onboarding
  #fan-art          - community creativity
  #clips            - short-form gameplay highlights
#game
  #bug-reports      - structured bug template
  #suggestions      - feature requests, voted by reactions
  #lfg              - looking-for-group, squad finder
  #trading          - if your game supports trading
#support            - moderator-only triage
#partners           - content creator partner access
```

Add role-gated channels for VIP GamePass owners as an extra perk (the
Roblox account-to-Discord link via Roblox Connect gives you ownership
check).

## Growing the Discord

### Phase 1: seed (0 → 100 members)

- **Invite in-game.** Use a TextChat welcome message with the invite
  link. Put the link on your experience description, Creator page,
  and in-game community tab.
- **Cross-promote** from other Discords you're already part of
  (respecting their rules — never cold-advertise).
- **Events: launch Q&A.** Get your earliest players in a voice chat
  and answer questions. Creates founder-relationship that sticks.

### Phase 2: grow (100 → 10k members)

- **Target Discord servers 3k–20k members.** Big enough to move the
  needle, small enough that the mod team might do a shoutout.
- **Befriend server owners first.** Don't spam your link — become a
  regular, then ask.
- **Content creator partnerships.** Send codes / VIP access to 1–3
  Roblox YouTubers / TikTokers with engaged audiences (not just
  follower count).
- **Roblox Events / community partnerships.** Roblox occasionally
  runs creator partnership programs. Keep an eye on the DevForum
  Announcements for calls.

### Phase 3: retain (10k+)

- **Weekly cadence.** Fixed slot for patch notes, fixed slot for
  community spotlight, fixed slot for partner spotlight.
- **Mods.** Promote trusted members into mod roles. Unpaid, but give
  them early access to features as the perk.
- **Feedback pipelines.** Use a bot like `#suggestions` with voting;
  publicly acknowledge top-voted suggestions even if you can't build
  them.

## Content creator partnerships

Effectiveness rank (high to low for Roblox):

1. **TikTok short-form.** Best discovery for casual genres. Creators
   with 50k–500k followers are the sweet spot — bigger ones are
   pricier per view.
2. **YouTube mid-form (5–15 min).** Best for tutorial/showcase
   content. Roblox-focused channels are the target.
3. **Twitch streams.** Smaller reach than YT but higher engagement
   per viewer. Sponsoring a stream is cheap and the VOD lives on.
4. **Instagram Reels.** Diminishing returns vs TikTok for Roblox
   audiences but easy to repurpose.

### Partnership offers (common deal types)

- **Codes + Robux kit**: send the creator 5,000–20,000 R$ worth of
  items and a promo code they can give viewers (great for VIP
  GamePass push).
- **Affiliate revenue share**: 10–20% of code redemptions via
  trackable link.
- **Flat sponsorship**: $500–$5,000 per video depending on creator
  size. Typical rate for Roblox YT: ~$10–$25 per 1k views.
- **Event host**: pay creators to host an in-game event.

### Metrics to track

- **Cost per acquired player** (CPA): total spend / unique new
  visitors from that creator
- **Day-1 retention of acquired players**: most important proxy for
  partnership quality
- **Lifetime value of acquired players**: total Robux spent / 30d
  after acquisition
- Aim for **CPA < LTV**. If CPA is $0.30 and 30-day LTV is $0.60,
  it's profitable.

## Impact on Discover algorithm

Remember: the Discover algorithm **does not count** ad, search, or
social traffic for ranking purposes. So a successful TikTok push:

- Helps **cold-start** and **burst** a new release
- Puts eyes on the experience
- But does NOT by itself move your algorithmic rank

You still need strong organic retention (qPTR, Deep-PTR, 7-Day
Play Days) to compound from the burst. Plan your partnership pushes
around a content update that has proven retention — otherwise the
burst fizzles.

## Concrete Numbers / Examples

- Sweet spot for Discord partnership targets: **3k–20k members**
- Typical Roblox YT sponsorship: **$10–$25 CPM**
- Effective TikTok follower range: **50k–500k**
- Common partnership kit: **5k–20k R$ in items + promo code**
- Success threshold: **CPA < 30-day LTV**

# Ethical Monetization

**Status:** stub

## Summary

Core principles for Roblox's young audience:

1. **No loot boxes with real-money purchase** (Roblox ToS — paid random items require odds disclosure and pity systems)
2. **No pay-to-win** that makes free players feel unable to compete
3. **No FOMO** targeting children
4. **Free players have a complete, enjoyable experience**
5. **Save time, don't gate content**
6. **Paid random items** require mandatory odds disclosure + weighted chance + pity code

COPPA compliance considerations apply — design for the <13 audience.

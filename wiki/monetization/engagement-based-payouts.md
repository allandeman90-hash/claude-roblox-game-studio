# Engagement-Based Payouts (EBP)

**Status:** stub

Revenue tied to Premium subscriber playtime. Largely replaced by **Creator Rewards** as of the 2025 policy change. Creator Rewards pays 5 R$ per Active Spender session (10+ min, first 3 of day), plus 35% on first $100 of audience expansion.

# Premium Benefits

**Status:** stub

## Summary

Perks for Roblox Premium subscribers. Detect via `Players.LocalPlayer.MembershipType == Enum.MembershipType.Premium` (or equivalent server-side check). Common perks: exclusive chat color, +N% rewards, Premium-only rooms or cosmetics.

Premium playtime feeds the Engagement-Based Payouts metric, now largely replaced by Creator Rewards.

# Robux Price Tiers

**Status:** stub

## Summary

Common player Robux purchase tiers (what a player typically has in their account):
- **80 R$** ($0.99)
- **400 R$** ($4.99)
- **800 R$** ($9.99)
- **1,700 R$** ($19.99)
- **4,500 R$** ($49.99)
- **10,000 R$** ($99.99)

Design in-game item prices so they fit just below a tier — e.g., a 75 R$ item is affordable after an 80 R$ purchase with 5 R$ left over. A 99 R$ item fits in a 400 R$ tier. "Charm" prices: 49, 99, 199, 499, 999, 1999, 4999.

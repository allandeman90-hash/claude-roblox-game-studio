# Developer Exchange (DevEx)

**Status:** stub

## Summary

Roblox's program for converting earned Robux to USD. Current rate (2025+): **$0.0038 per earned Robux** (up from $0.0035). Minimum cashout: 30,000 R$ (~$114 USD).

Revenue model:
- Player buys 400 Robux for ~$5
- Platform takes 30% cut on DevProduct/GamePass purchases
- Developer earns ~280 Robux from that 400 R$ sale (pre-tax)
- At DevEx, ~$1.06 per $5 player purchase

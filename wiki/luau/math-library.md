# `math` Library

**Status:** stub

Standard math with Luau extensions: `math.clamp(v, min, max)`, `math.noise(x, y, z)` (Perlin), `math.sign(x)`, `math.round(x)`, `math.lerp(a, b, t)`, `math.map(v, a, b, c, d)`.

Plus standard: `math.huge`, `math.pi`, `math.random`, `math.floor`, `math.ceil`, `math.abs`, `math.min`, `math.max`, `math.sqrt`, `math.sin`/`cos`/`tan`, etc.

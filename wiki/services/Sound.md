# Sound

**Status:** stub

Individual sound instance. Parent to a Part or Attachment for 3D positional audio; parent to SoundService or PlayerGui for 2D. Key properties: `SoundId`, `Volume`, `PlaybackSpeed`, `Looped`, `RollOffMode`, `RollOffMinDistance`, `RollOffMaxDistance`, `SoundGroup`.

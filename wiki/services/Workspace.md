# Workspace

**Status:** stub

The 3D world container. Parents all physical Parts, Models, Terrain, and the CurrentCamera. Replicated to clients (clients see the same workspace as the server, modulo StreamingEnabled).

Key properties: `StreamingEnabled`, `StreamingMinRadius`, `StreamingTargetRadius`, `Gravity`, `CurrentCamera`.

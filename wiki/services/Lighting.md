# Lighting

**Status:** stub

Global lighting and atmosphere. Properties: `Ambient`, `Brightness`, `ClockTime`, `FogEnd`, `FogStart`, `EnvironmentDiffuseScale`. Descendants: `Sky`, `Atmosphere`, `Clouds`, and post-processing effects (`BloomEffect`, `ColorCorrectionEffect`, `DepthOfFieldEffect`, `SunRaysEffect`, `BlurEffect`).

Lighting modes: `Voxel`, `ShadowMap`, `Future` (best visuals, highest cost), `Compatibility` (legacy).

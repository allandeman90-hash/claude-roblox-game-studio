# SoundService

**Status:** stub

Global audio configuration and `SoundGroup` container. Routes all Sound instances through their group hierarchy. Settings: `AmbientReverb`, `RollOffScale`, `DistanceFactor`.

Organize sounds into groups (Music, SFX, UI, Voice, Ambient) for independent volume control.
